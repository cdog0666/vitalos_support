Frequently asked questions

How do I log a meal?

Tap the + button, choose Meal, then pick breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack. You can search the food database, scan a barcode, or enter a food manually. Choose a serving size and tap add.

How do I scan a barcode?

In the meal logging screen, tap the barcode icon and allow camera access when askbarcode. If a product isn't found, you can add it manually.

How do I change my calorie or macro goals?

Go to Profile, then Goals. You can edit your daily calories, protein, carbs, and fat targets at any time.

Can I switch between imperial and metric units?

Yes. Open Profile and change Units. The app also supports English and Spanish.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Meals, water, workouts, and weight entries are saved on your device and sync automatically when you're back online.

Why aren't my steps showing?

VitalOS needs Motion & Fitness access. Go to iPhone Settings > VitalOS andss is turned on.

My data didn't sync to a new phone.

Log in with the same account you used before. Your data is pulled from your account when you sign in. Make sure you have an internet connection.

How do I export my data?

Open Profile and choose Export my data. You can download everything stored in you

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your data, and it cannot be undone. If you can't access the app, email us from your account email address and we'll delete it for you.
